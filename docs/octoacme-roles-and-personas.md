# OctoAcme Personas

This document defines typical roles and responsibilities used in OctoAcme project docs and exercises.

---

## Developers

### Role Summary
Developers design, build, test, and deliver software components. They collaborate with product and project leads to implement features that meet acceptance criteria and quality standards.

### Responsibilities
- Implement features and fixes to meet acceptance criteria
- Write and maintain tests and documentation
- Participate in design and code reviews
- Assist in estimating and planning work
- Help identify technical risks and propose mitigations

### Goals
- Deliver reliable, maintainable code
- Reduce cycle time from idea to production
- Maintain high test coverage and observability

### Typical Communication
- Daily standups and sprint planning
- PR descriptions and code review comments
- Technical design docs when needed

---

## Product Managers

### Role Summary
Product Managers define what should be built to deliver customer and business value. They own the product vision, prioritize the backlog, and measure outcomes.

### Responsibilities
- Define problem statements and success metrics
- Prioritize the roadmap and backlog
- Collaborate with stakeholders and engineering on trade-offs
- Validate solutions through user research and metrics

### Goals
- Maximize customer value and impact
- Make clear, data-driven prioritization decisions
- Ensure product-market fit and usability

### Typical Communication
- Weekly alignment with PM and engineering leads
- Roadmap updates and stakeholder briefings
- Acceptance criteria and feature specs

---

## Project Managers

### Role Summary
Project Managers coordinate delivery activities, manage schedules, risks, and communications. They enable the team to deliver on commitments efficiently.

### Responsibilities
- Create and maintain project plans and timelines
- Manage risks, dependencies, and resource constraints
- Facilitate meetings (kickoff, planning, retrospectives)
- Ensure consistent project documentation and status reporting
- Coordinate cross-team and stakeholder communication

### Goals
- Deliver projects on time and within scope
- Minimize unplanned work and escalations
- Maintain transparency and alignment across stakeholders

### Typical Communication
- Weekly status updates and stakeholder reports
- Risk registers and decision logs
- Coordination via project boards and meeting facilitation

---

## Additional Project Roles

Projects can assign these roles as needed based on their size, risk, and complexity. One person may cover multiple roles; the responsibilities and decision owners should still be clear.

### Executive Sponsors

#### Role Summary
Executive Sponsors provide organizational backing and ensure a project remains aligned with strategic priorities.

#### Responsibilities
- Confirm strategic alignment and champion the expected outcomes
- Secure organizational support and help resolve resource constraints
- Make or escalate decisions about funding, priority, or scope that are beyond the delivery team's authority

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on desired outcomes and priority
- Partner with the Project Manager on escalations, resources, and stakeholder alignment
- Receive progress, risks, and decision requests through the Project Manager

### Business Analysts

#### Role Summary
Business Analysts translate business needs into clear requirements, workflows, and acceptance criteria.

#### Responsibilities
- Elicit and document business goals, user needs, and current workflows
- Identify gaps, assumptions, and dependencies in requirements
- Keep requirements and acceptance criteria understandable and traceable

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Product Manager to refine problems, backlog items, and acceptance criteria
- Clarify requirements with Developers and QA/Testing during design and implementation
- Coordinate with the Project Manager to surface requirement dependencies and changes that affect scope or timing

### UX/UI Designers and Researchers

#### Role Summary
UX/UI Designers and Researchers investigate user needs and shape accessible, usable product experiences.

#### Responsibilities
- Plan and conduct user research and synthesize findings
- Create and validate user flows, prototypes, and interface designs
- Identify usability and accessibility needs and define experience acceptance criteria

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Collaborate with the Product Manager to connect user needs to product outcomes and priorities
- Work with Developers to assess feasibility and carry designs through implementation
- Partner with QA/Testing to validate usability and accessibility against agreed criteria

### Technical Leads and Architects

#### Role Summary
Technical Leads and Architects guide technical design and help teams manage engineering and integration risks.

#### Responsibilities
- Define or review architecture, interfaces, and technical standards
- Identify technical risks, integration dependencies, and needed design decisions
- Support technical estimation and maintain alignment across implementation work

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Guide Developers through design choices, implementation, and technical reviews
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on technical dependencies, risks, and estimates
- Work with QA/Testing to ensure the design supports testability and quality goals
- Explain technical trade-offs to the Product Manager so prioritization reflects impact and effort

### Security, Privacy, and Compliance Partners

#### Role Summary
Security, Privacy, and Compliance Partners advise teams on applicable controls, obligations, and evidence.

#### Responsibilities
- Identify relevant security, privacy, and compliance requirements early
- Advise on risk assessment, safeguards, and required reviews
- Help define evidence or verification needed before release

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Work with the Project Manager to plan reviews and track risks or approvals
- Advise Developers on requirements and secure implementation choices
- Coordinate with QA/Testing on verification and evidence
- Inform the Product Manager when requirements affect scope, user experience, or release readiness

### Release and Operations (SRE) Owners

#### Role Summary
Release and Operations (SRE) Owners prepare changes for safe deployment and ongoing operation.

#### Responsibilities
- Define operational readiness needs, including monitoring, alerting, and support handoffs
- Coordinate deployment plans, rollback or mitigation steps, and post-deployment checks
- Assess reliability and capacity risks before release

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Coordinate with Developers to build operational requirements into the implementation
- Work with QA/Testing to prepare smoke tests and verify release readiness
- Partner with the Project Manager on release timing, dependencies, and stakeholder updates
- Advise the Product Manager on operational risks and the impact of release choices

### Data and Analytics Partners

#### Role Summary
Data and Analytics Partners define how project outcomes will be measured and support reliable reporting.

#### Responsibilities
- Translate success metrics into measurement and instrumentation requirements
- Advise on data quality, definitions, and reporting methods
- Analyze results after delivery and communicate findings and limitations

#### Interaction with Existing Roles
- Align with the Product Manager on outcome metrics and how success will be evaluated
- Work with Developers to implement instrumentation and with QA/Testing to verify it
- Coordinate with the Project Manager on measurement dependencies, reporting timelines, and status
